Mechanical & Robotics Portfolio

I have had the opportunity to do a range of amazing and fun challenges and mechanical projects inside and outside of university.

Tesla | Mechanical Design Engineer Intern

Design, prototype and testing thermal coolant components on current vehicles (Cybertruck) and future vehicle (Cybercab and one other unannounced).

Big Dipper Lunar Regolith Acquisition Challenge | Mechanical Engineer

I led a team to design and prototype ROMEO, a lunar regolith acquisition device that uses electromagnetism for efficient sample collection, winning 1st place in a national challenge aimed at innovating excavation methods for lunar exploration.

3D Printer Hotend Challenge | Mechanical Engineer

Led a team of five in developing an efficient 3D printer hotend, improving flowrate performance by 127% through thermal analysis, design, and testing.

Engineering Innovation Challenge 2023 | R&D Mechanical Engineer

Led a team to win 1st place in the prestigious Engineering Innovation Challenge, designing a flood-proof road solution, and becoming the first international team in the competition's history to achieve this honor.

Monash Nova Rover | Mechanical Engineer

Contributed to the design, prototyping, and assembly of payloads, rover subsystems, and excavation mechanisms. Led the team to place 1st in Australia and 2nd in the world in prestigious rover competitions.

Monash Nova Rover x NASA | Mechanical Engineer

Led the design and manufacturing of lunar excavation and thermal testing mechanisms, collaborating with NASA's Swampworks on real-world exploration mission solutions.

WARMAN | Mechatronics Engineer

Led a team of engineers in designing, prototyping, and testing a solution for the Warman Competition, focusing on rapid prototyping, design iteration, and project management.

Hoisting Platform | Mechanical Design Engineer

Led the design and development of a hoisting system for wind turbine engineers, focusing on motor selection, load calculations, FEA, and the design of mechanical components.

TOM | Mechanical Engineer

Worked on enhancing a wheelchair for a woman with Scoliosis and Parkinson's to improve posture and reduce health risks through innovative, inclusive design.
