Mechanical & Robotics Portfolio

I have had the privilege of working on a diverse range of mechanical and robotics projects, both within and outside of university. From leading teams in prestigious engineering competitions to collaborating with global organizations like Tesla and NASA, I've gained hands-on experience in designing, prototyping, and testing innovative solutions to real-world challenges. These experiences have fueled my passion for engineering and provided valuable insights into the power of collaboration, creativity, and practical problem-solving.

Tesla | Mechanical Design Engineer Intern

Designed, prototyped and tested thermal coolant components on current and future vehicle (Cybercab and Cybertruck RWD), owning seven thermal coolant bundles from RFQ to DFM.

Big Dipper Challenge (ELO2, NASA) | Mechanical Engineer

Led a team to design and prototype ROMEO, a lunar regolith acquisition device using electromagnetism for efficient sample collection, winning 1st place in a national lunar excavation challenge.

3D-Printer Hotend Optimization | Mechanical Engineer

Led a team of five in developing an efficient 3D printer hotend, improving flowrate performance by 127% through thermal analysis, design, and testing.

Engineering Innovation Challenge | Mechanical Engineer

Led team to win 1st place in the Engineering Innovation Challenge, designing, prototyping, and testing a flood-proof road solution, receiving the award from the Deputy Prime Minister of Singapore.

Monash Nova Rover | Mechanical Engineer

Contributed to the design, prototyping, and assembly of payloads, rover subsystems, and excavation mechanisms. Represented the team globally, and contributing to it placing 1st in Australia and 2nd in the world in 2022 and 2023.

Monash Nova Rover x NASA | Mechanical Engineer

Led the design and manufacturing of lunar excavation and thermal testing mechanisms, collaborating with NASA's Swampworks on real-world exploration mission solutions.

WARMAN | Mechatronics Engineer

Led a team of engineers in designing, prototyping, and testing a solution for the delivery of a payload across a chasm, focusing on rapid prototyping, design iteration, and project management.

Hoisting Platform | Mechanical Design Engineer

Led a team in the design and development of a hoisting system for wind turbine engineers, focusing on motor selection, load calculations, FEA, and the design of mechanical components.

TOM | Mechanical Engineer

Worked on enhancing a wheelchair for a woman with Scoliosis and Parkinson's to improve posture and reduce health risks through innovative, inclusive design.
